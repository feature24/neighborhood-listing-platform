# Stakeholders, Facts, and Assumptions

**Project:** Neighborhood Listing Platform
**Course:** CIS 3610
**Owner:** Jacob De Luna
**Method:** Individual project using simulated discovery. No real participants were contacted.

## Stakeholder Groups
- Property manager: Manages rental listings and updates information on available properties for rent.
- Local café owner: Promotes their business to people around the neighborhood.

## Known Facts
- The assignment requires discovery questions, personas, user stories, acceptance criteria, and a Week 8 MVP plan.
- The lab permits a simulated interview.

## Assumptions
- A property manager may need an easier way to keep listing information accurate.
- A café owner may want clearly labeled promotions that link to their business.
- Visitors may benefit from property filters and mobile-friendly pages.
- Listing and sponsor content may need review before publication.

These assumptions are planning ideas, not findings from real interviews.
